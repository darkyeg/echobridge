EchoBridge for Linux - removes headphone sound that leaks into your microphone

QUICK START
1. Install: run ./install.sh from this folder (no administrator rights needed), or on Debian
   and Ubuntu install the .deb with: sudo apt install ./echobridge_*.deb
2. Open EchoBridge from your applications menu, or run: EchoBridge
3. Open Setup and choose:
   - Microphone: your headset microphone.
   - Headphones: the device whose sound leaks into the microphone.
   - Send the clean microphone to: EchoBridge Microphone.
4. In Discord or any call app, choose "EchoBridge Microphone" as the microphone. Keep your
   headphones as the call app's speaker.
5. Press Start on the Live page. The status turns green when your microphone is protected.

No virtual cable to install: EchoBridge creates the virtual microphone itself, and it
appears while EchoBridge is running. Turn on "Start when you sign in" in Setup so that it is
always there when you open a call app.

REQUIREMENTS
PipeWire, which current Ubuntu (22.10 and later), Debian 12, Fedora and Arch use by default.
Apps that speak PulseAudio (Discord, Chrome, Firefox) work through pipewire-pulse. If
EchoBridge says it cannot reach PipeWire, check:   systemctl --user status pipewire

THE TRAY ICON
KDE, XFCE, Cinnamon and MATE show it. GNOME needs the "AppIndicator and KStatusNotifierItem
Support" extension; without it, EchoBridge still runs, and you can reopen its window by
starting it again.

DIAGNOSTICS
EchoBridge --list-devices
EchoBridge --leak-test 10 --report leak.json

Everything is processed locally in memory. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
