EchoBridge - removes headphone sound that leaks into your microphone

QUICK START
1. Install VB-CABLE from https://vb-audio.com/Cable/ (free). Extract it, run VBCABLE_Setup_x64.exe
   as administrator, choose Install Driver, and restart if asked.
2. Run EchoBridge-Setup.exe, or open the portable EchoBridge.exe. EchoBridge itself needs no
   administrator rights and no other software.
3. Open Setup and choose:
   - Microphone: your headset microphone.
   - Headphones: the device whose sound leaks into the microphone.
   - Send the clean microphone to: CABLE Input.
4. In Discord or any call app, choose CABLE Output as the microphone. Keep your headphones as
   the call app's speaker.
5. Press Start on the Live page. The status turns green when your microphone is protected.

Do not make CABLE Input your Windows playback device. It only receives the clean microphone.

WHAT THE BUTTONS DO
Start / Resume: removes the leak.
Pause: your microphone still reaches the call app, unchanged.
Turn off: the call app hears nothing from EchoBridge.
Closing the window keeps EchoBridge running in the notification area. Quit it from the tray icon.

CHOOSE HOW THE LEAK IS REMOVED
Clean voice: never alters your voice. Best for a wired leak, such as a headset on a combined
audio jack.
Adaptive: removes the leak and keeps your voice clear while you talk.
Strong: removes the most, but can muffle your voice while music plays.

Noise removal is Off by default because call apps remove noise too. Standard removes steady
noise like Chrome does. AI uses a small speech model that keeps your voice natural; it adds
30 ms of delay.

THE METERS
Headphone playback, Microphone and Clean output show levels in dB. While only music plays, the
clean output should stay low. When you talk, it should follow your voice.

FIND OUT WHERE THE LEAK COMES FROM
Open Leak test. Play music on your headphones and stay silent.
Step 1: wear the headset normally and press Record.
Step 2: press both earcups into a pillow, keep everything plugged in, and press Record.
If covering the earcups does not lower the leak, the leak is electrical. A USB audio adapter
or a USB headset gives the microphone its own ground.
Save report writes numbers (never audio) to %LOCALAPPDATA%\EchoBridge\reports.

STARTUP
"Start with Windows" opens EchoBridge in the notification area when you sign in.
"Protect the microphone when EchoBridge starts" starts echo removal right away. Otherwise your
microphone passes through unchanged until you press Resume.
Settings are saved automatically in %LOCALAPPDATA%\EchoBridge\settings.json.

UNINSTALL
Use Windows Settings > Apps. This removes the program and its startup entry. Your settings
are kept.

PRIVACY
Audio is processed on this PC, in memory. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
